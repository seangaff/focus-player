Project: Focus player

Music player rewarding productivity with dopamine while allowing you to think.

Main concept: play music only when user is typing to induce dopamine when productive, fade music out when they stop to help collect thoughts/ relax/regroup.

Potential features:

- Background music, white or brown noise that plays when no keys pressed
- Options for musics playback
    - Each key mapped to a note
    - Each key press plays a “note” of a song
    - Music fades in and out with key presses
        - Speed/volume of playback matches speed of key presses?
- Key goal is to have music naturally flow with typing, should pause when typing stops.
    - Scaling volume with WPM sliding window may not be enough? Velocity of typing may be more important.


Tech Design:
- Monitor: keylogger to detect key presses and measure WPM
- Player: manages playback of music based on WPM or note mode, single song, playlist, and/or background track.
- Session: tracks current song choices, WPM, time spend type, chars typed, breaks taken
- Timer: Used to optionally set pomodoro style working and break intervals.
